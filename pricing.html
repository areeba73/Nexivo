Everything You Need to Know About Nexivo

Got questions? Here are the answers institutions and organizations ask most, and if you need more, we’re a message away.

Nexivo is a learning management system that educational institutions and organizations can purchase and use to manage their own courses, learners, trainers, batches and enrollments from one centralized platform.

Schools, colleges, academies, coaching centers and training institutes, as well as companies, corporate training teams and other organizations. Each one uses Nexivo to manage its own learning and training activities.

Learner management, batch and class management, courses and training programs, trainers and staff, online and mock tests, results and performance tracking, attendance, grades and organized records, all from one dashboard.
